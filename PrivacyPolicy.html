Effective Oct 24, 2026 · Sparelux for iPhone

Sparelux is a local shelf for spare stuff. Photos, price guesses, swap cards, pickup windows, and cash ticks live in the app folder on your phone. There is no account, and this build does not upload your list to a Sparelux server.

Stays here Item titles, notes, photos you attach, estimate ranges, handoff cards, ledger rows, nickname.
Not a marketplace We do not broker buyers, move money, or message strangers for you. Your numbers are notes.
01

What the app actually stores

On this device only:

  • Shelf items (title, category, wear, low/high, path, notes, optional photo file)
  • Estimate results you choose to put on the shelf
  • Swap cards and pickup notes you write in Circulate
  • Cash rows in Ledger and an optional nickname

Text rows live in app preferences; photos sit in a private Documents subfolder. Delete a row to clear that row. Uninstalling Sparelux clears both.

02

Camera and photos

Camera and photo library open only when you tap Take photo, From library, or Attach photo. Sparelux uses the image you pick so it can sit with that item. We do not scan your whole library in the background.

03

Network, accounts, ads

Core use does not need a login or an online account. There is no Sparelux cloud for your shelf list and no analytics SDK in this build.

If the system shows a network prompt for OS updates or other tooling, that is outside Sparelux’s shelf data.

04

Kids and common sense

Sparelux is a household clear-out notepad, not a social product for children. Do not put other people’s private contacts into notes if you would not want that text sitting in an unlocked phone.

05

Changes

If this page changes, the date at the top moves. Keep using the app only if the new wording still fits how you want your spare-stuff list handled.

06

Contact

Questions about this policy: use the App Store listing contact for Sparelux. We do not publish a mailing address on this page.
